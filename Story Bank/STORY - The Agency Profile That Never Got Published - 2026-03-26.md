# STORY — The Agency Profile That Never Got Published — Raw Capture
*2026-03-26*

*Raw language preserved. See also: [[INSIGHT - Creative Range Managed Down by Small Systems - 2026-03-26]] · [[REFERENCE - Peterson - Creativity and Systems - 2026-03-26]]*

---

I didn't feel like a failure. Mostly I was different. I didn't belong.

My ideas were taken but often I was told "we don't work that way." I was tolerated because of the results but not the work methodology. And when I brought up new ideas I could feel it bothered their order. When I answered things differently it didn't make sense to them.

Here's a story. When I worked for this agency, the first thing they wanted to do was create a profile for me for marketing. They asked questions like: what do I like, who do I look up to, what was my thing.

I couldn't resist noticing — they all answered generic, very ethno-centric answers. They answered from what they knew of their small world. Like who do they look up to — it was a local or provincial figure. While I was inspired by Simon Sinek or Ruud Janssen, world leaders in their fields.

What was my weakness? Night food markets. But they never experienced it because they all live in Quebec so they didn't even know what it was. They answered generic things like chocolate.

Who would you like to interview? They named some local or provincial figure. I told them my brother that passed away.

Guess what — nothing of what I said was ever published. Because I didn't fit into their system.

---

It's also about creativity. Because I was proposing thinking out of the box to grow — not just be stuck in their small circle thinking. That bothered them. And they didn't understand that way of thinking.
