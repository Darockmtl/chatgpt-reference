# STORY — Track and Field — I Failed — Confidence Without Measurement — 2026-03-20

*Raw voice capture. Preserved verbatim.*
*See also: [[STORY - Track and Field - I Failed - Confidence Without Measurement - 2026-03-20]] (Speaker Academy copy)*

---

I just have to remember the story of me being younger and doing track and field and sprinting the 100m and I thought I was pretty good and I wasn't doing bad but the funny part is that I have somebody from high school — I think his name was John, have to look it up. He said I had a certain confidence, I had speed. He was massive legs and I said that's no problem, so we did the race and guess what? Yeah, that's right. I failed.

And you know what — that confidence, I was cocky. I was confident. I knew what I was doing and I did train hard, but John was 6 foot 1 and every stride had so much length over me. I didn't even think about that. I realize now it was just that I thought I was one of the top five, and that's a reality you have to come to — you have to be able to measure where you are. It's not a failing, but you also have to analyze where you are to see where you're going to go and how you can train to become better.
