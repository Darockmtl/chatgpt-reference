Hi my name is AH. Let me tell you what a Master of Ceremonies does.

He is a timekeeper making sure the show runs on schedule.

He makes sure that your key messages are highlighted.

He makes smooth transitions between subjects.

He entertains the guests to liven up the room.

YEAH I don't do any of that....

I create time bubbles with stories that stretch time or shorten time.

I dig deep to make everyone discover what the messages mean to them.

I design a map so the audience can go on a trip and take pit stops, explore off the beaten paths or find secret places that make each leg even better.

I work with their energy and take them where they need to be.
