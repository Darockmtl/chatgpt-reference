## The Concept

Every speaking moment has a felt duration — the time the audience experiences versus the time on the clock. A nervous speaker rushes through material the audience needed to sit inside. An underprepared speaker fills time the audience stopped needing five minutes ago. The room always knows the difference.

The term that came out of Anthony's MC work: **time bubbles**.

A time bubble is a contained moment — a story, an image, a silence, a question — that suspends the audience's awareness of clock time and pulls them fully into the room. When it works, ten minutes feels like two. When it fails, two minutes feels like ten.

This is not a metaphor for engagement. It is a structural decision. Anthony chooses when to open a bubble and when to close it — and that choice is made in real time, reading the room, adjusting to the energy in front of him. Twenty years of MC work across wildly different industries is what makes that reading accurate.
