**Author:** Benjamin Ball Associates

**Where it fits in Speaker Academy:** DESIGN module (before speech structure frameworks like TTT/PREP/POWER)

### 2. Narrative Thrust Test & So/But/Then Connective Tissue

**Original clipping:** Best Presentation Structure. 9 Tips To Structure Presentations

**Original source:** https://benjaminball.com/blog/how-to-structure-your-presentation/

**Author:** Benjamin Ball Associates (Sarah Plater)

**Key insight:** Rearrange your slides. If they still make sense, you have no narrative thrust—you have a list. So/But/Then creates connective tissue that forces narrative logic.

**Where it fits in Speaker Academy:** DESIGN module (diagnostic for structure) + LiveLab feedback language

**Reference document:** `/01 - Online Course/DESIGN/SOURCE - Business Presentation Frameworks/REFERENCE - Narrative Thrust Test & Connective Tissue (So-But-Then).md`

**Processing includes:** The test, how thrust comes from cause/effect/problem-solution, So/But/Then framework, examples of weak vs. strong, teaching strategy, decision tree, common pitfalls, freebie concepts

**Product potential:** One-page diagnostic checklist, worksheet/audit tool, 5-min demo video, 2-hour workshop

### 3. Monroe's Motivated Sequence (Persuasion)

**Original clipping:** BLUF, AIM, AIDA 17 Powerful Presentation Frameworks Guide

**Original source:** https://benjaminball.com/blog/guide-to-powerful-presentation-frameworks/

**Author:** Benjamin Ball Associates

**Key insight:** Five steps: Attention → Need → Satisfaction → Visualization → Action. Visualization is the differentiator—helps audience feel the better future. Stronger than AIDA for speeches.

**Where it fits in Speaker Academy:** DESIGN module (persuasion track); connects to sales page argument work

**Reference document:** `/01 - Online Course/DESIGN/SOURCE - Business Presentation Frameworks/REFERENCE - Monroe's Motivated Sequence (Complete Deep Dive).md`

**Processing includes:** Five steps with depth, why Visualization is the differentiator, vs. AIDA comparison, full worked example, teaching notes, decision tree, common pitfalls, freebie concepts

**Product potential:** One-page template, workbook with examples, 5-video series, email course, 2-hour workshop

### 4. SUCCESs Framework (Stickiness Diagnostic)

**Original clipping:** BLUF, AIM, AIDA 17 Powerful Presentation Frameworks Guide

**Original source:** https://benjaminball.com/blog/guide-to-powerful-presentation-frameworks/

**Author:** Benjamin Ball Associates

**Key insight:** Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories. NOT a structural framework—it's a diagnostic. Use as finishing pass after you've built structure.

**Where it fits in Speaker Academy:** DESIGN module (stickiness layer; finishing pass, not primary structure)

**Reference document:** `/01 - Online Course/DESIGN/SOURCE - Business Presentation Frameworks/REFERENCE - SUCCESs Framework (Stickiness Diagnostic).md`

**Processing includes:** Six elements with depth, critical distinction (diagnostic vs. structural), full example of diagnostic application, teaching notes, decision tree, common pitfalls, freebie concepts

**Product potential:** One-page checklist, worksheet, 6-video series, workbook, email course

**Key insight:** Two alternative frameworks for vocal delivery. RAMP (Rate, Articulation, Modulation, Pause) vs. 4Ps (Projection, Pace, Pitch, Pause). Positioned as context for Vocal Dials approach.

**Where it fits in Speaker Academy:** DELIVER module (confirmation that Vocal Dials territory is well-established; reinforces why Vocal Dials needs distinctive framing)

**Reference document:** `/01 - Online Course/DELIVER/REFERENCE - RAMP & 4Ps (Vocal Frameworks Context).md`

**Processing includes:** Both frameworks explained, quick comparison, how they relate to Vocal Dials, why Vocal Dials is different (mixing approach vs. list), teaching strategy, decision tree, freebie concepts
