## FRENCH DRAFTING — PATTERN FLAGGED AGAIN

Anthony caught Claude translating English to French multiple times during this session. Pattern: English skeleton with French words on top. Examples flagged:

- "L'animateur bilingue qui entre dans votre événement" = English syntax with French words
- "je propose un appel court" = translated, not conceived in French
- Qualifiers like "les événements qui comptent" = English rhetorical move dressed in French

Rule reinforced: **think in French from the start**. If you check whether a line was conceived in French or translated — and the answer is translated — throw it out. Start over.

Anthony also asked Claude to respond in French for part of the session to break the translation pattern. Claude maintained professional Quebec French register (no Québécois familiarity, no "checker", no pompous tone).
