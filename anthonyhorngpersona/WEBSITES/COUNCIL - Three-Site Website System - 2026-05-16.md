# COUNCIL — Three-Site Website Strategy Decision
*Date: 2026-05-16*
*Panel: skeptic + pre-mortem + chief-of-staff*
*Source: STRATEGY - Three-Site Website System - Anthony Horng - 2026-05-16.md*

---

## The structural truth

All three critics, working independently, hit the same load-bearing failure from different angles: **this is not a website strategy, it is an identity document with URLs attached.** The Skeptic names it at the positioning layer. The Pre-Mortem names it at the execution layer. The Chief of Staff names it at the priority layer. Three vantage points, one structure: **the author is using the document to feel like a coherent practitioner, not to convert a buyer.** Every other weakness — the incoherent proof surface, the missing reel, the placeholder third site, the bilingual agency repulsion — is downstream of that single substitution.

---

## 1. Recommended action

**REVISE — and revise hard.**

Collapse the three-site strategy into a single two-week ship list for animateur.ca. Do not reopen the anthonyhorng.com / holdtheroom.com architecture until: (a) the positioning line has been tested against three past planners in their own words, and (b) animateur.ca has converted at least one inquiry attributable to the rebuild.

The bones are not worthless — animateur.ca has a live ranking and a real buyer pool. But the strategy as written burns budget and months defending a self-concept the market has never confirmed. Killing it wastes the asset; proceeding as-is ships the avoidance.

---

## 2. Top 3 risks

**1. The positioning line is untested and likely a re-skin.**
"Lives in the event, not on the stage" is contrastive grammar every working MC could claim. If three past clients describe Anthony as "bilingual MC who handles corporate stuff smoothly," the hero line is fiction and the rebuild converts at the 2024 baseline. *(Skeptic + Pre-Mortem converge here — this is the highest-stakes single risk.)*

**2. Architectural energy is mis-allocated away from the income surface.**
animateur.ca has buyers, a ranking, and named fixable defects. The strategy spends equal weight on a site whose role the author admits is "unresolved" and a domain that is currently DNS. Twelve months and ~$14K can disappear with zero booking impact. *(Chief of Staff + Pre-Mortem.)*

**3. The proof surface contradicts the promise.**
TELUS/iA/TEDx mixed with Moisson Laurentides/Canada Day, with photos "insufficient" and reel "status unknown," signals the wrong tier to the exact buyer the positioning tries to win — corporate/agency producers booking $50K+ bilingual galas. The architecture makes a credibility claim the assets cannot underwrite. *(Skeptic, echoed by Pre-Mortem's "more of a community guy now" moment.)*

---

## 3. Top 3 reasons in favor

**1. animateur.ca is a real asset with a real funnel.** Live #4 ranking, live buyer pool, French MC market with thin competent competition. This converts today and can convert better with named, scoped fixes.

**2. Anthony has unusually clear self-diagnostic vocabulary.** The "Unresolved" list, the admission the positioning hasn't been tested, the flagging of photo and reel gaps — the document is honest enough to be revised. Most strategy docs at this stage aren't.

**3. The instinct to separate FR-buyer, name-search, and EN-buyer surfaces is structurally sound.** A single site cannot serve all three audiences without compromise. The architecture is wrong in sequencing and weight, not in basic shape — revision, not demolition.

## Raw critic outputs

### SKEPTIC

**Strongest argument against:**
This is a three-site system designed to solve a problem the author hasn't proven exists: that buyers care about the difference between an MC who "lives on the stage" and one who "lives in the event." No planner has ever said that. The entire architecture is scaffolding built to justify a self-concept, not to win bookings. The author admits the positioning line has never been tested with a planner. That's not an "open hesitation." That's the foundation being made of sand, and the author building three floors on top of it anyway.

**Three specific weaknesses:**
1. The differentiator is a re-skin of every MC's pitch. "Most MCs live on the stage. I live in the event." Every working MC claims they're a partner, a producer, an event whisperer. The line is contrastive grammar dressed up as insight. It commits to nothing. "Live in the event" is unfalsifiable.
2. The author's incentive bleeds through the architecture. anthonyhorng.com is "the only page where all four pillars connect" — built for the author to feel coherent, not for a buyer to make a decision. "Not a conversion site. No booking button at the top" is self-flattery dressed as strategy.
3. The proof surface is incoherent and the author knows it. "TELUS/iA/TEDx mixed with Moisson Laurentides/Canada Day." Combined with "Photos insufficient" and "Reel status unknown," the positioning is making promises the assets cannot deliver.

**Audience it will actively repel:**
Corporate event producers at agencies booking $50K+ bilingual galas. They land on the worldview argument, see no booking path, and bounce. The intellectual frame signals "this person will be precious on site, will push back on the run-of-show, will want a creative conversation when I need a yes." Repelled.

**One unanswerable question:**
Name three planners who booked you in the last 18 months who would describe what you do — unprompted — as "living in the event" rather than "great MC." If the answer is zero, the positioning isn't a discovery about the market. It's a story the author is telling himself.

### PRE-MORTEM

**Cause of death:**
The three-site system died because Anthony spent twelve months building infrastructure for a positioning line that no planner ever validated, so when the rebuilt sites went live in October 2026 they converted at the same rate as the stale 2024 version — and the budget for the rebuild was already spent.

**Timeline:**
- Month 1: Title tag cleaned. Reel embed deferred (reel "status unknown"). Discoverability spec not written — MUDGIRL prep took over. Position at #4 held on inertia.
- Month 6: Rebuild kicked off six weeks late. anthonyhorng.com launched with untested positioning line. First three planners emailed asking "do you do bilingual MC for corporate?" — the exact question the site was supposed to answer pre-emptively. Google AI Overview still cited competitors. One agency intermediary asked whether he was "more of a community guy now."
- Month 12: Inquiry volume on animateur.ca flat year-over-year. anthonyhorng.com: 2,100 sessions, 11 inquiries, 0 bookings. holdtheroom.com still a placeholder. ~$14K and ~400 hours spent. One measurable outcome: the author could answer "what do you do?" with a sentence he liked.

**Signal that should have killed this before launch:**
Item #1 in the "Unresolved" list was "Positioning line never tested with a planner." A single 20-minute call with three past clients asking "what did you tell your boss I do?" would have returned "bilingual MC who handles corporate stuff smoothly" — not anything resembling the hero line. That call was never made because the positioning felt true to Anthony.

**Author's blind spot:**
Anthony refused to see that anthonyhorng.com was built for himself. The actual buyer was a 34-year-old comms coordinator at an insurance company with 8 minutes. She needed a price range, a reel, and a recent corporate logo she recognized. The site gave her none of those above the fold. Anthony called this an "architecture decision." It was an avoidance.

### CHIEF OF STAFF

**Stated goal:** Build a three-site system where animateur.ca converts French MC buyers, anthonyhorng.com closes name-search visitors, holdtheroom.com converts English MC buyers.

**Actual goal served:** Maintain a defensible intellectual posture about what each site should be, so the author can keep thinking about positioning instead of shipping the income surface.

**The gap:** animateur.ca is the stated primary income surface — live ranking, live buyer pool, fixable named defects. Yet the document spends roughly equal architectural energy on anthonyhorng.com (role flagged as "unresolved") and holdtheroom.com (DNS placeholder, no timeline). The positioning vector points at the system. The goal points at the cash register. Not the same direction.

**Decision being avoided:** Whether animateur.ca is a business or a brand exercise. Wedding, photos, "full rebuild deferred" — three costumes for the same decision: the author has not committed to the FR MC market as a real revenue channel.

**Soft-goal substitution:** "I want conversion" has become "I want a coherent three-site worldview." "Practitioner's personal site," "person is the frame," "worldview is the content" — essayist's language, not operator's language.

**Go/No-Go: No-go.** Collapse the document: animateur.ca gets a two-week ship list with dates; anthonyhorng.com gets a one-line role or a kill date; holdtheroom.com gets removed from the strategy doc until there is a build trigger. Re-open only after animateur.ca has converted one inquiry attributable to the rebuild.

**The drift, named:** You are using site architecture to avoid deciding whether the French MC business is your priority or your fallback.
