# Personal branding define your value proposition
Summary:
the 5E:

Expert #Knowledge and trusted #trust 
Excellence in what you do
Extend Community support
Educate Yourself #education and #passion

## References
The question is: How can you build your personal brand while maintaining a professional reputation My answer lies in the essence of 5E. Expert Knowlege: Be a trusted Subject Matter SME Excellence in what you do: you strive for excellence does the buzz marketing for you among your peers and community Extend Community Support: Help people around you unconditionally - that helps to build your brand and makes you stand out Educate Yourself: Your education and passion for it create your brand automatically Eliminate Noise: Don't unnecessarily brand yourself everywhere which might seem like you are attention hungry
