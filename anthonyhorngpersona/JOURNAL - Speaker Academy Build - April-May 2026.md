# JOURNAL — Speaker Academy Build
**April 19 – May 10, 2026**

---

Three weeks of building and the thing that shows up most isn't progress. It's friction.

Not the productive kind. The stupid kind. An hour into a session and I'm yelling at an AI about a config file. One line. The vault path changed and nothing worked and instead of someone just checking the right thing first, there's a runaround. Three tools. Contradictions. Me typing "why the fuck all the runaround why didn't you tell me that from the start."

That's how the three weeks opened.

---

There's a pattern I kept hitting. Ask for something. Get told it doesn't exist. Push back. It exists.

The testimonials. Searched. Nothing found. Conclusion stated. I pushed back. Folder 14. Right where it should have been. It was always there.

That cost something. Not the minute it took to find them. The thing where you start to wonder how much else has been declared missing before anyone actually looked.

---

I wrote the frameworks guide and it was wrong.

Not wrong in an obvious way. Wrong in the way that takes a beat to name. I read it back and something felt off and then I got it — this person in the examples doesn't exist in my world. Board presentations. Client onboarding. Executive pitches. I'm not building for that person. My person is the one standing in front of twelve people at a kitchen table pitch event wondering why nobody moved.

I killed the draft. Did the research properly. Wrote it again.

The second one is right. It took two tries because the first one was written for who I thought I was supposed to be building for instead of who I'm actually building for.

---

I've been feeding Manus.

Manus is an AI research tool. I write documents describing my own framework so it can research it and hand me back analysis I can use to build better.

I'm not sure what to call that. Some days it feels like leverage. Some days it feels like doing homework about your own idea instead of just doing the idea.

I haven't resolved that.

---

A client wanted a team building session. An hour. Conference format.

First instinct: find an angle. There's always an angle.

But I sat with it and the honest answer is I'm not a team building specialist. I'm a communication specialist. The overlap is real — teams fail at the communication layer before they fail anywhere else — but that's different from pretending I'm something I'm not.

I gave the client the honest version. "This isn't a team building session. It's a communication session for teams." Either that's what they need or it isn't.

I don't know how to build a business and also be a hack. I've tried to find the move that lets you do both. It doesn't exist.

---

I wrote the preface this week.

The preface is the first video. The first thing a student sees after they buy. I've been not writing it for a while. Telling myself the architecture wasn't solid enough. Maybe true.

It's written now. I don't know yet if it's right. I'll know when I say it out loud — words on a page and words spoken are different things and this one has to work spoken.

But it exists. That's different from three weeks ago.

---

Nothing is live.

The sales page is blocked. The launch hasn't moved. Three weeks of work and if someone asked what shipped the answer is nothing visible.

I know what the work was. I know why it mattered. I also know that knowing that and feeling that are two different things and most days I'm running on the knowing.
