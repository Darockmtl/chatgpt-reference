# STORY — MUDGIRL Training Line — You Can't Start Now
*Raw capture — May 15, 2026*

At MUDGIRL, from the mic, I ask the crowd: how many of you trained hard for the last six months?

Pause.

Then: how many of you trained the last few seconds?

Laugh. Then the point lands: you can't start now. What you did before this moment is what carries you through it.

Used as a crowd warm-up line at start line. Also the live proof of the Outside-In communication tool — the body learns before the moment arrives. By the time you're in the hard conversation, the training either happened or it didn't. You can't install it at the door.
