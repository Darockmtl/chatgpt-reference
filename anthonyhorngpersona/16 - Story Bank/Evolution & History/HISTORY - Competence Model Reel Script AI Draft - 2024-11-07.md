Based on your notes, here is a 5-minute script for your public speaking reel based on the Four Stages of Competence:

"Hello everyone, today we are going to explore the journey of learning public speaking through the lens of the Four Stages of Competence model. Just like in public speaking, as a novice, you may feel overwhelmed and lost, relying on instructions and guidance to take any action. This stage is known as unconscious incompetence, where you are unaware of your lack of skills.

As you progress to the advanced beginner stage, you start recognizing familiar switches. This is similar to conscious incompetence in public speaking, where you become aware of the skills you lack and the areas you need to improve. Moving to the competent stage, you understand the layout of the 'room' of public speaking. This is conscious competence, where you can deliver a speech with effort and concentration, using established procedures.

Transitioning to the proficient and expert stages, you anticipate the needs of your audience and act instinctively. This is unconscious competence, where your movements on stage become almost automatic, guided by experience and intuition."
