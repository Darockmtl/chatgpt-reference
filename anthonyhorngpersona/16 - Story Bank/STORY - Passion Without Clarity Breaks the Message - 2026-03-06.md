During a workshop, I was speaking with one of the students because we were talking about designing their message.

I asked that person, what exactly is your product?

Instead of telling me what her project was, she went straight into her pitch. She started telling me that this is very innovative. Then she started giving me facts. Then she started giving me arguments and saying that not many people think like this, or that people are thinking differently, but because she is one of the few that thinks this way, this is the future.

So she basically turned my question, tell me what your project is about, into a pitch.

At that point I lost all context and detail. I had to reframe her and say, you're not selling to me, I just want to know what it is, your project or your product, so I'll be able to help you bring it.

If she wanted to go into more detail later, like why she thinks like this and everything else, that would be different.

But this connects to the idea that passion can be a dangerous thing, because now the message didn't pass through. It was too complicated, and I didn't even want to listen anymore.
