# SEED — LinkedIn MC Credibility Post

---

## WHAT THIS IS

A parked seed for a LinkedIn post output type designed to position Anthony as the MC to hire. Requires dedicated session with event context and client feedback as input before it can be built.

---

## THE IDEA

A LinkedIn post format where Anthony feeds in:
- Context of a specific event he MC'd
- Client feedback or testimonials from that event

Output is a post that builds MC credibility — not a case study, not a sales pitch, but a post that makes the right reader think: this is the person I need in the room.

---

## WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT FROM THE EDUCATIONAL POST

The educational post earns saves through teaching. This post earns trust through proof. The mechanism is social proof delivered through Anthony's observational voice — not through listing credentials or quoting testimonials directly.

The goal is not to impress. The goal is to make a stranger trust that Anthony can hold their room.

---

## WHAT NEEDS TO BE FIGURED OUT IN THE DEDICATED SESSION

- What raw inputs does Anthony provide (event type, moment that happened, client language verbatim)
- How much of the client feedback gets used directly vs filtered through Anthony's voice
- What does the reader need to feel after reading it — confidence to hire, or curiosity to learn more
- Where does this post sit in the funnel — warm lead trigger or cold audience awareness
- Format: story-led, or observation-led, or hybrid
- Does it name the client/event or stay anonymous

---

## CONTEXT NEEDED BEFORE BUILDING

Anthony needs to bring at least one real event example with:
- What the event was and who the client was (or a description if anonymous)
- A specific moment or challenge from that event
- The client's actual words in feedback — verbatim if possible

Without real material this post cannot be built. Do not invent.
