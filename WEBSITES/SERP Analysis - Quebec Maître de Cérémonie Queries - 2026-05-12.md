## Part C: Comparison with round 1 findings

- **Eklosion's Dominance is Weaker:** Unlike the "animateur" universe where Eklosion frequently held the #1 spot, their performance in the "maître de cérémonie" universe is softer. They rank #2 for "maître de cérémonie Québec" but drop to #7 for accent-free variants and #15 for "maître de cérémonie gala".
- **Different Competitors Emerge:** The "maître de cérémonie" SERPs introduce new competitors not seen prominently in Round 1, such as Frédéric Charpentier (`fredericcharpentier.com`), Randy Johnston (`randyjohnston.ca`), and GM MC² (`iamgmmc.com`).
- **Wedding Officiant Pollution:** The "maître de cérémonie" queries (especially for Montreal) are heavily polluted by wedding officiants (e.g., Rose Gold Celebrations, Wedding Wire directories). This split was less pronounced in the "animateur" queries, which leaned more toward general entertainment or corporate.
- **France-Based Intrusion:** Queries with event-context qualifiers like "congrès" and "entreprise" are severely impacted by France-based results (e.g., Jocelin Haas, WeChamp, Sabine Vandebroek). This suggests a lack of strong, localized Quebec content targeting these specific long-tail "maître de cérémonie" keywords.
- **animateur.ca Visibility:** Anthony Horng (`animateur.ca`) appears at #10 for "maître de cérémonie Montréal" and #15 for "maître de cérémonie québécois", showing some visibility but trailing behind his performance in the "animateur" SERPs.

## Part E: Pattern observations
