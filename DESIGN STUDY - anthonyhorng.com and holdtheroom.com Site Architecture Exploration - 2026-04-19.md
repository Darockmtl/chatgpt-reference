# DESIGN STUDY — anthonyhorng.com + holdtheroom.com Site Architecture Exploration
*2026-04-19 — Session-long design study using cassandraworthy.com as the reference site. Four prototypes built. Not launch copy. Thinking tool for the post-MUDGIRL rebuild session.*

*Session conducted in claude.ai desktop, 2026-04-19. No active share link — this file is the capture.*

---

## WHY THIS SESSION EXISTED

Anthony asked: *"If I give you a website, can you copy the format or be inspired by it to make my website?"*

Reference site provided: `https://cassandraworthy.com` — Cassandra Worthy, keynote speaker, Founder/CEO of Change Enthusiasm Global.

Explicit framing from Anthony: thinking tool, not launch copy. *"Adapt it, useful as a thinking tool."*

The Master Plan v2 (2026-04-18) explicitly defers the anthonyhorng.com full rebuild to post-MUDGIRL (post-Sep 19). Blockers 1 and 2 (photos, wedding decision) gate the rebuild. The governing-layer session flagged in [[INSIGHT - Personal Brand Arc Tension and Governing Layer Gap - 2026-03-19]] has not happened.

This session was a design exploration done knowing all of the above — a way to see what the content looks like in different shapes before the real rebuild session happens.

---

## HOW THE THINKING SHIFTED

The session produced four prototypes, each one a response to a re-framing of what anthonyhorng.com is actually for.

### Prototype 1 — Cassandra structure cloned literally

Built with Cassandra's full section structure: agency bar, hero with rotating endorsements, press row, argument section, video, origin story, three-pillar framework, three-product row, testimonial wall, logo strip, book block, final CTA.

Filled with Anthony's vault material. Positioning line (*"Most MCs live on the stage. I live in the event."*) in the hero slot.

**Three structural tensions flagged:**

1. **Hero concept slot expects a trademarked IP.** Cassandra repeats *Change Enthusiasm®* five times on the page. The structure is built to brand a concept, not state an argument. Anthony's positioning line is an argument, not a brand asset.
2. **Three-product row assumes one buyer.** Cassandra's Keynote / Beyond the Keynote / CEG all serve the same buyer at different commitment levels. Anthony's three pillars (MC / Speaker Academy / Workshops+Keynote) serve three different buyers who don't overlap. An MC planner reading a course offer on the same page reads dilution, not range.
3. **Proof layer built around celebrity.** Cassandra has Simon Sinek, Ken Blanchard, Michael Bungay Stanier. Anthony has transformation-level client quotes that name specific mechanisms (the Chanelle Turgeon Gervais quote). Different proof architecture.

### Reframe 1 — "It's an umbrella"

Anthony: *"This is the brand umbrella — then it would go to Speaker Academy or holdtheroom or animateur.ca."*

This resolved the three-buyer tension structurally. The site's job becomes routing, not selling.

### Prototype 2 — Umbrella, language-first router

Built as a short router page: hero with wordmark + positioning line, two language tiles (EN / FR) as the primary CTA, one "who this is" section with argument, one proof section, logo strip, footer.

No service menu. No booking form. No methodology framework. The umbrella doesn't sell — it routes. Every selling conversation happens on the destination site in the chosen language.

### Reframe 2 — Pressure test

Anthony asked to push further. Three real challenges surfaced:

1. **Language-first routing costs the bilingual ICP a click.** A bilingual internal-communications lead who lands on anthonyhorng.com is making a *buyer* decision (does this person do MC?), not a language decision. Asking her the language question first is asking the wrong question. Buyer-first or single-language-with-FR-toggle might serve the ICP better.
2. **"The umbrella doesn't sell" breaks for hot referrals.** A direct MC buyer referred by a TELUS contact lands on the umbrella, has to pick a language, navigate to holdtheroom.com, find the contact form, fill it in. Four steps for a lead that would have been one step on a direct site.
3. **Phase 3 absence is structurally wrong if the site is a Phase 3 build.** The Master Plan says anthonyhorng.com is "personal brand hub long-term" — which is keynote territory. Building without keynote presence means building a Phase 1 umbrella that needs to be rebuilt in Phase 3.

### Reframe 3 — "Push further — what's the optimal state?"

Anthony: *"Push further — what would be the good way of doing it, pretending everything is developed and approved."*

Built the Phase 3 optimal-state thinking: anthonyhorng.com becomes a keynote speaker site structured like Cassandra's, because the job is the same as hers (book a keynote). The umbrella framing was a Phase 1 solution to a Phase 3 site.

### Reframe 4 — "Not keynote, no agency yet"

Anthony corrected the framing. The optimal state in his world still doesn't include keynote or a bureau relationship.

This was the critical correction. It invalidated both the Cassandra-clone (wrong buyer) and the umbrella (wrong problem) and the keynote-site (wrong phase).

### Prototype 3 — Practitioner's personal site

Reference analogs shifted from Cassandra Worthy to Vinh Giang / Shadé Zahrai / Marie Forleo. What those sites have in common:
- The person is the frame
- One hero, one argument, one worldview
- Offerings are present but not the lead
- Proof is mechanism-specific
- Conversion is relationship (email list), not transaction

The site's three jobs, in order:
1. Establish who Anthony is in one screen
2. Make the worldview visible (communication is design)
3. Route to the right next step without selling

Eight sections: header, hero, argument, methodology, origin story, proof, routes, orbit (email), footer.

### Prototype 4 — holdtheroom.com (EN MC conversion site)

The Cassandra structural pattern that didn't fit anthonyhorng.com *does* fit holdtheroom.com. One buyer, one language, one decision. The MC planner needs to move from "promising" to "I'm sending the inquiry."

Key shifts from anthonyhorng.com:
- Reel is the hero, not a CTA among several
- Argument compressed to two paragraphs, planner-framed punchline instead of category-theory punchline
- Four confirmed service buckets from the Master Plan (corporate / galas / bilingual / conferences)
- Process section answers *"what's it going to be like working with you?"*
- Agency/producer kit for intermediaries (Roxanne Bacha equivalents per Master Plan)
- Real qualifying inquiry form (name, org, date, event type, audience size, goal) — not just email capture

## WHAT THE SESSION PRODUCED

### Durable discoveries

**[[INSIGHT - Governing Layer Gap as a Physical Design Problem - 2026-04-19]]** — the March 19 insight was abstract. This session made it concrete: the gap is not just a content-routing problem, it's a structural design problem. Different buyers produce different sites. An attempt to serve three buyers from one site physically bends the structure in a way that's visible in a prototype.

**[[INSIGHT - anthonyhorng.com Is a Practitioner's Personal Site - 2026-04-19]]** — not an umbrella, not a keynote site, not a conversion site. A personal site for a practitioner who runs multiple offerings. The frame is the person. The argument is the reason to care. Routing happens at the bottom, after the argument has done its work.

### Design studies (prototypes as thinking tools)

Four design studies, preserved as embedded HTML below. Not launch copy. Each one was built to test a specific structural hypothesis.

### Meta-pattern confirmed

The recurring meta-pattern Anthony has flagged across sessions held here too. The first prototype pattern-matched on the reference site instead of reasoning about what kind of site Anthony actually needs. The reframes (one through four) were each corrections to that pattern-matching. The final answer required dropping the reference site's structural logic entirely and starting from Anthony's actual conditions.

The prototypes are useful because they *show the category error* physically — you can see the three-buyer routing problem in Prototype 1, the umbrella-has-no-conversion problem in Prototype 2, the Phase-3-placeholder problem in the optimal-state reasoning. The thinking tool did its job.

## WHAT'S NOT RESOLVED

- **The positioning line is still untested.** The vault is explicit: *"Most MCs live on the stage. I live in the event."* needs a live test with a planner before it moves to website copy. All four prototypes assume the test goes well. If it doesn't, the worldview section on anthonyhorng.com needs rewriting — which is three-quarters of the site.
- **The Ben Nemtin origin moment is still raw.** The origin story on anthonyhorng.com runs on *pattern* (20 years of rooms) rather than *scene* (one specific moment). Pattern is intellectually strong but doesn't hit emotionally the way Cassandra's $5B-acquisition-drowning-in-booze story does. [[STORY - Ben Nemtin - See You On The Road - Raw Capture]] is the emotional anchor in the making but isn't developed yet.
- **Logo tier mixing.** The 10-logo strip mixes TELUS / iA / TEDx tier with Moisson Laurentides / Canada Day tier. Either curate harder (top six corporate only) or segment by type.
- **Wedding decision still open.** Per Master Plan v2, wedding-on-animateur.ca is not decided. Same question will apply to holdtheroom.com. Not included in this study's holdtheroom.com prototype by default.
- **The governing-layer session referenced in [[INSIGHT - Personal Brand Arc Tension and Governing Layer Gap - 2026-03-19]] still has not happened.** These prototypes give that session a concrete artifact to react to, but they do not replace it.
