# Copywriting style
to  inspire the style language and the humour and tone of the message.

 The worst thing you can do when running a workshop isn’t being boring.

(Or even peeing your pants.

That would be rough, but luckily I haven’t done that in several years. I mean decades.)

The actual worst thing is to OVERLOAD them with CONTENT:

- 3 frameworks 
- 6 acronyms
- 23 “best practices”

No matter how good your content is:

If you TEACH too much, they’ll REMEMBER nothing.

So, what’s the “right amount”?

My rule of thumb is:

Per 90 minutes of workshop time:

- 1 Big Idea
- 1 Framework
- 1 Exercise (with feedback)

This is my Signature Workshop Structure. 

I’ve run over 300 workshops, and taught this to 60+ speakers, so I can safely say:

It will work for ANY 90-min workshop you have to run.
