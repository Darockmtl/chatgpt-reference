# INSIGHT — anthonyhorng.com Is a Practitioner's Personal Site
*2026-04-19 — The site's actual category, determined after four failed reframings in the design-study session.*

---

## THE DISCOVERY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

anthonyhorng.com is not an umbrella. It is not a keynote speaker site. It is not a conversion site. It is a **practitioner's personal site** — a home for a person who runs multiple offerings, where the person is the frame and the offerings are expressions of the frame. The reference analogs are Vinh Giang, Shadé Zahrai, and Marie Forleo (pre-book). Cassandra Worthy is *not* the right reference, because her site is a single-buyer keynote conversion site optimized to close a keynote booking. Anthony's site is optimized to turn a stranger who heard about him into someone who is in the orbit — and then the orbit does the selling over time.

---

## HOW THE CATEGORY WAS DETERMINED

Four reframings in the April 19 session each tried a different category for anthonyhorng.com. Each one failed for a specific structural reason.

**Category 1 — Single-buyer conversion site (Cassandra clone).** Fails because the three-product row forces three buyers onto one page. The MC buyer reads dilution. The course buyer reads off-track. The keynote buyer doesn't exist yet.

**Category 2 — Umbrella / routing site.** Fails because (a) language-first routing is the wrong first question for a bilingual ICP, (b) hot referrals hit a four-step path, (c) Phase 3 absence means it needs rebuilding in Phase 3.

**Category 3 — Keynote speaker site (optimal-state pretend).** Fails because it requires keynote + bureau + book + Phase 3 audience, none of which exist. Also throws away MC and Speaker Academy buyer pipelines that currently produce revenue.

**Category 4 — Practitioner's personal site.** Works because it accepts that the page cannot convert all three buyers and stops trying. The conversion event becomes *relationship* (email list, stay-in-orbit signup) — a low-commitment commitment that keeps every buyer type in the audience without forcing any single funnel.

---

## WHAT A PRACTITIONER'S PERSONAL SITE DOES

Three jobs, in order:

**Job 1 — Establish who the person is in one screen.** Wordmark, tagline, positioning line, one-sentence identity claim. A visitor should know what kind of person this is within 10 seconds.

**Job 2 — Make the worldview visible.** The argument the person actually holds, in their own voice, long enough to earn the reader's attention. This is the section that filters the audience — someone who doesn't care about the worldview self-selects out, which is the correct outcome.

**Job 3 — Route to the right next step without selling.** Offerings are visible but not the lead. They appear after the worldview has done its work. The primary conversion is relationship (email list) — the destinations are where transactions happen.

---

## WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

- **Does not sell.** No pricing. No booking form. No service menu.
- **Does not route as its primary job.** The umbrella version failed because routing-as-purpose is too thin to sustain a homepage.
- **Does not impersonate a keynote site.** The Cassandra structural pattern (single concept, single buyer, book + bureau + final CTA) doesn't fit a multi-offering practitioner.
- **Does not list services across pillars.** The three-product row is the structural symptom of the governing-layer gap. A practitioner's site solves this by listing *routes* (go to animateur.ca for MC, go to Speaker Academy for courses) rather than *services* (MC / Course / Workshop / Keynote as menu items).

---

## THE REFERENCE SHIFT

The session started with cassandraworthy.com as the reference. That was wrong.

The correct references are sites like **Vinh Giang's personal site, Shadé Zahrai's personal site, and early Marie Forleo** (pre-book, pre-MarieTV scale). These sites share:

- Person-as-frame, not product-as-frame
- One hero, one argument, one worldview
- Offerings present but not the lead
- Mechanism-specific proof (what the work produces), not celebrity endorsement
- Conversion is relationship (email list, newsletter, community), not transaction

This is a meaningfully different category of site from keynote speaker sites. Keynote sites are optimized to close a keynote booking. Practitioner sites are optimized to keep the audience in orbit while the person's multiple offerings do their own conversion work.

---

## WHY THIS CHANGES THE MASTER PLAN'S LONG-TERM FRAMING (SUBTLY)

The Master Plan v2 describes anthonyhorng.com as *"EN MC surface now; personal brand hub long-term."*

The "EN MC surface now" is Phase 1 and is correct — that's what the pre-reel sprint is doing (reel embed, minimal language updates).

The "personal brand hub long-term" is the piece this insight refines. *Personal brand hub* can mean two very different things:

- **Hub as in routing center** (the umbrella framing — failed above)
- **Hub as in home** (the practitioner framing — correct)

The practitioner framing is more modest than "hub" suggests. It's not a center that feeds traffic to destinations. It's a home that exists alongside the destinations. A visitor to animateur.ca doesn't need to visit anthonyhorng.com first. A visitor to Speaker Academy doesn't need to visit anthonyhorng.com first. Each destination stands alone. anthonyhorng.com is the home for visitors who came looking for *Anthony Horng the person* — via referral, via content, via search on his name.

This is a smaller scope than "hub." It is also more defensible, because it doesn't require the destination sites to depend on the hub for traffic.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR THE REBUILD

When the post-MUDGIRL rebuild of anthonyhorng.com happens, the design brief is:

- **Structure:** 8 sections (header / hero / worldview / methodology / origin / proof / routes / orbit / footer)
- **Primary conversion:** email list signup
- **No services, no pricing, no booking form**
- **Routes section at the bottom:** MC route → animateur.ca + holdtheroom.com / Course route → Speaker Academy + Académie Prends la parole
- **Worldview section is the longest** — this is where the site earns attention
- **Origin story runs on pattern (20 years across industries) until Ben Nemtin moment is developed**
- **FR version is a bio stub, not a mirrored site** — French buyers route to animateur.ca or Académie directly

Prototype 3 in [[DESIGN STUDY - anthonyhorng.com and holdtheroom.com Site Architecture Exploration - 2026-04-19]] is the working design brief.
