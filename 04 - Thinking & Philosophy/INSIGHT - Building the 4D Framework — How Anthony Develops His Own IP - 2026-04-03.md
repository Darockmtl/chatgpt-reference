# Building the 4D Framework — How Anthony Develops His Own IP

## Observation

The 4D Mastery Cycle (Discover, Design, Deliver, Develop) wasn't received as a finished framework — it was built iteratively through dialogue. Anthony tested versions, pushed back on weaknesses, asked for the right word ("find something that starts with D"), and sought scholarly validation. The process reveals something about how he develops his own intellectual property: through friction, iteration, and the need for both practical coherence and academic grounding.

## What the Process Reveals

He doesn't accept a framework because it sounds good. He asks whether it holds up theoretically. When the prototype was presented, his next move was to find its weaknesses — and when "Refine" was proposed as the fourth step, he pushed back until it was reworded to fit the pattern. That's not cosmetic. That's someone who understands that naming shapes meaning, and that the form of a framework communicates before its content does.

The decision to seek scholarly backing (Kolb, ADDIE, Argyris, Ericsson) wasn't about credibility performance — it was about confirming that the framework was discoverable, not invented. He was looking for whether what he built already existed in established learning theory. It did. That matters to how he positions his work.

## Implication for Positioning

Anthony builds frameworks from inside out — experience first, structure second, validation third. The 4D cycle started from how he already teaches and works backward to what that structure is. This is the opposite of adopting someone else's model and teaching from it.

This is a positioning asset. He didn't copy a framework — he built one that independently maps to multiple established learning theories. The story of how it was built is part of the IP's credibility.

## Connection to Personae Work

The 4D framework is the course OS. How it was built is the origin story. Both are worth developing — the framework for the course, the origin story for the positioning. See [[REFERENCE - Anthony Horng Project Context]] for the broader Personae context.
