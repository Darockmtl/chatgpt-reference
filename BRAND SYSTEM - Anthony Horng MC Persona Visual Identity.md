## Colors — Four Values (Shared with Speaker Academy — April 2026)

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Dominant surface | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Secondary surface | Warm gray | `#F2F0EB` |
| Signal + accent | Yellow | `#F5D800` |
| Type + CTAs | Black | `#111111` |

### Usage Rules

**White** — primary background for all pages, posts, and content. Default surface.

**Warm gray** — secondary background only. Use for body copy slides, breathing room in long-form content, and alternating section backgrounds. Never the primary surface.

**Yellow** — accent only. One element per layout maximum. High-signal, use on what matters most.

**Black** — all type, CTAs, and structural elements.

## Typography — Three Fonts, Two Contexts (Shared with Speaker Academy)

### Logo — Bebas Neue
- Reserved for wordmark use only

### Content (everything you control)

| Role | Font | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Headings | Plus Jakarta Sans | 800 |
| Body | Inter | 400 regular / 700 emphasis |

Both fonts are free on Google Fonts and native in Canva.
