# Post-Mortem Guide — Discover → Design → Deliver
**Client:** Borea | **Date:** 2026-03-25 | **Language:** English

> Run 5–6 questions based on conversation flow. Don't run all 10 in sequence — let it breathe.
> Questions marked 🎯 are highest-yield for social proof — note exact phrasing, don't rephrase during the call.

---

## Phase 1 — Open with energy

**1.** "From your side, is there something from the event that people are still talking about?"

**2.** "Was there a moment where you felt the room shift in tone — something you noticed?"

---

## Phase 2 — Before / after contrast

**3.** "Before we started working together, were there things you were concerned about going into this event? And did those concerns end up materializing?"

**4.** 🎯 "If this event had gone the way events like this typically go — without this kind of prep work upfront — what do you think would have been different?"

---

## Phase 3 — Inside the methodology

**5.** "We worked in three phases: first we dug into your world, then we built the narrative thread, then we executed on the ground. Was there one of those steps that felt different from what you'd experienced before?"

**6.** "Were there things you expected me to handle that I didn't? And on the flip side, were there things I brought that you didn't expect?"

---

## Phase 4 — Mine for their language

**7.** 🎯 "If someone asked you what I do exactly — someone who's never worked with an MC or a script designer — what would you tell them?"

**8.** 🎯 "If you had to put a label on what we did together — hosting, consulting, content design, something else — what would you call it?"

---

## Phase 5 — Close with refinement

**9.** "If we did this again, are there things you'd cut? Things you'd add?"

**10.** "When you think about what made this event feel different — how much of it do you put on the content, the delivery, the energy in the room — or a mix of all three?"
