# QUOTE — Francesca Psychology — Dunning-Kruger and Hedge Words

> The Dunning-Kruger effect says that the more intelligent you are, the more competent you get, the more you are aware of your incompetence, the more you are aware of your own ignorance. And so you often use hedge words such as "to the best of our knowledge" or "as far as we know" or "as far as science knows." Whereas the reverse Dunning-Kruger effect is people who are excessively confident in their knowledge and their abilities — often those who don't have the most knowledge or the best abilities.

**Source:** Francesca Psychology (video transcript — @francescapsychology)
**Use when:** The context is authenticity, credibility, the courage of saying "I don't know," or the difference between performed confidence and earned confidence.
