## Raw Draft (verbatim from source)

Im AH and I speak across Canada as host [[INSIGHT - Speaking Is Facilitation From the Front of the Room - 2026-03-27|facilitator]] and speaker for corporate and public events.
But I was not always like that.

I used to own a dance studio and when the troupe had to change costume at a show at a live event I was stuck with the mic trying to fill up time so I tried to keep them entertained. I was nervous babbling and hoping that [[INSIGHT - Preparation vs Training — The Athlete Framework for Speakers - 2026-03-03|high energy would keep them engaged]].

I started in the clubs and bars and it was to entertain and at first it was a thrill but it started to feel empty because it was on the moment and it didn't bring satisfaction. And it was repetitive.

I was used to simply repeating what I was told and it was good. [[INSIGHT - From Performer to Trailblazer - The Evolution Behind the Work - 2026-03-06|I started to feel fake]].

I studied in theatre where I learnt a whole new world and to create that stage presence to spark emotions and spark my own creativity. I was motivated.

So I went into motivational mode where everything was "you can do it" — and I did that for a while as race announcer and at any event — till I discovered that once again it didn't bring value except beyond the present moment of telling people to do it but I didn't have any actions that could last.

I discovered that my talent was in crafting speeches and talks and that one needed to find value.

I studied Event design from the Event Design Collective, a world renowned organization, and what I learnt is that when you craft something you need to be intentional. Design thinking.

Then the corporate world where messages and marketing was the key word but often I would find that those messages were more just buzz words and trying to create the wow factor. So it was like going back to the entertainment.

That's when I started learning design thinking, how to craft talks, discovered storytelling and its power to move audiences, the art of rhetoric — logos pathos and ethos — to convince and persuade, and stage presence.

Through trial and error and lots of practice I've developed different techniques to adapt messages to any audience — and that was born [[COURSE PHILOSOPHY - Speaker Academy - 2026-04-04|Speaker Academy]]. To gain confidence, structure your ideas, and stage presence to deliver with impact.
