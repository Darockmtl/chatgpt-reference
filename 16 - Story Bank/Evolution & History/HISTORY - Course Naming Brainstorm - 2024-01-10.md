# HISTORY — Course Naming Brainstorm and Early Communicative Design Framing

> [!NOTE] HISTORICAL ARTIFACT — January 2024
> This file documents where the course name search stood in early 2024, before Speaker Academy was locked. It also captures an early AI-assisted attempt to frame the intellectual territory of the course under the label "Communicative Design."
>
> **When to pull this file:**
> - Building the origin narrative for the keynote or sales page
> - Writing content that shows the journey — the search, the iterations, the thinking before clarity arrived
> - Demonstrating that Speaker Academy wasn't named in an afternoon — it was the result of a real intellectual process
>
> **Claude notes (March 2026):**
> Two names were in play here: "Speak Up Academy" and "Present With Purpose Academy." Neither made it. "Speak Up" is too timid — it implies people aren't speaking at all, when your real buyer is already speaking and needs to be heard differently. "Present With Purpose Academy" is closer in spirit but locks the scope to presentations, which the final course explicitly rejects.
>
> The "Communicative Design" framing is AI-generated synthesis from a January 2024 ChatGPT conversation. It is not a citable source and cannot become a reference note. But look at what it was reaching for: audience-centered, clarity, storytelling, emotional connection, interactivity. That vocabulary is almost identical to the language that eventually grounded Speaker Academy's positioning. The label didn't survive. The instincts behind it did.
>

**Communicative Design** — early framing attempt (AI-generated, January 2024):

1. **Audience-Centered Approach** — Understanding the audience's needs, interests, and background. Tailoring content, language, and visual elements to suit the audience.

2. **Clarity and Simplicity** — Simplifying complex concepts, avoiding jargon, using clear visual aids.

3. **Visual Communication** — Visual elements like images, charts, graphs, and colors to support the verbal message.

4. **Storytelling and Narrative Flow** — Structuring the presentation in a narrative form to make it relatable and engaging.

5. **Interactivity and Engagement** — Questions, audience participation, multimedia elements.

6. **Consistency and Branding** — Consistent style in colors, fonts, and layouts aligned with brand identity.

7. **Emotional Connection** — Personal anecdotes, shared values, addressing common concerns and aspirations.

8. **Feedback Incorporation** — Audience reactions used to refine and improve the presentation.
