## FONT

When choosing typography to represent your brand as a public speaking coach focused on empathy, growth, adaptability, and connecting deeply with your audience, it's important to select fonts that embody these characteristics.

### Typography Recommendations

**Primary Typeface (Headline/Display)**

- Sans-serif Option: Montserrat or Raleway — modern, clean, professional while remaining approachable
- Serif Option: Playfair Display or Lora — elegance and authority, adds warmth when paired with sans-serifs

**Body Typeface (Body Text)**

- Sans-serif Option: Open Sans or Roboto — versatile, highly legible, human touch
- Serif Option: Merriweather — readability with classic feel

**Combination Options**
- Montserrat + Open Sans: confident, friendly, approachable
- Raleway + Lora: elegant and professional
- Playfair Display + Roboto: tradition and modernity

**Considerations**
- Choose a typeface with a wide range of weights for flexibility
- Fonts should reflect brand personality — uplifting, yet grounded
- Ensure legibility across web, presentations, and printed materials
