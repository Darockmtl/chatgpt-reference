# POSITIONING SEED

## The Line

> "The Five Canons make you a better speaker. SA makes you a speaker."

## What It Means

Every communication system in the Western tradition — from Aristotle to Toastmasters — was designed for people who were already willing to stand up. The problem those systems solve is effectiveness.

SA solves a different problem. It starts before the decision to speak. It builds the foundation that every other system assumes you already have.

That's not a feature. That's a category distinction.

## Where It Can Live

- Course positioning / sales page
- "Why SA" content
- Keynote opening or closing
- Response to "how is this different from other public speaking programs?"
- Brand positioning foundation — as a core truth, not a tagline

## What It Is Not

This is not a line about being more empathetic or more human than competitors. It's a structural claim. SA begins earlier. That's it. The rest follows from that.
