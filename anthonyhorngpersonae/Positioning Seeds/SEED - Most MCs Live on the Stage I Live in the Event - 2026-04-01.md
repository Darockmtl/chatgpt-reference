# SEED — Most MCs Live on the Stage. I Live in the Event. — 2026-04-01

## The Line

> Most MCs live on the stage. I live in the event.

Emerged at the end of a full positioning session — 2026-04-01.
Raw. Unforced. Do not refine without good reason.

---

## Why It Works

"Live on the stage" captures exactly what the research confirmed — MCs perfecting their craft, their energy, their presence, their performance. The stage is their world. Their language is performance: energy, voice, charisma, connection.

"Live in the event" puts you inside the whole system. The audience. The strategy. The design. The tech team. The message. The before and the after. Not visiting it. Inhabiting it.

This is identity language, not craft language. That's the difference from earlier versions of this idea.

---

## What It Replaces / Sharpens

Earlier version from same session: *"Most MCs work on the stage. I work on the event."*
"Work on" is craft language. "Live in" is identity language. The upgrade was immediate and instinctive — keep it.

---

## Where It Fits

This is the governing sentence. The spine in one line.

Everything else — the CED, the DDD framework, the ICP positioning, the JVB posts, the LinkedIn strategy — sits underneath this line. It doesn't need to be explained. It opens a question the reader answers themselves.

---

## What It Does Not Say

- It does not position against agencies or event designers
- It does not claim to do event design
- It does not use "strategist" or "thought leader" as labels
- It does not require the buyer to accept a new framework before understanding the claim
