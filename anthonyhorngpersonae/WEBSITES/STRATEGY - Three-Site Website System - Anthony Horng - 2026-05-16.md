## THE THREE SITES — WHAT EACH IS

### animateur.ca
**Platform:** WordPress
**Role:** Quebec French MC market. Primary income surface. FR-only.
**What it does:** Converts a French-speaking buyer or intermediary into an inquiry.
**Current state:** Live but stale. Title tag still says "motivational speaker." No schema markup. No service-page architecture. Ranking #4 on "animateur bilingue Québec" on domain authority alone — not on content.
**What's in progress:** Minimal cleanup only (title tag, stale language). Full rebuild deferred post-MUDGIRL (post-Sep 19).
**Full rebuild scope (post-Sep 19):** Seven-component system — site, reel embed, info sheets ×4 (client FR/EN, agency FR/EN), LinkedIn, intermediary page, proof surfaces, booking flow. SEO architecture (service pages, schema, geographic targeting). AI Overview citation strategy.

**Open blockers for rebuild:**
- Photos — insufficient for a launch-quality rebuild
- Wedding — on or off animateur.ca? Not decided.

---

### anthonyhorng.com
**Platform:** Kajabi
**Role:** Credibility surface and routing hub. The only page in the system where all four pillars connect. When someone finds Anthony from any direction — referral, LinkedIn, event — this page makes the case for who he is, then routes them to the right destination.
**What it does:** When someone hears about Anthony and Googles his name, this is what closes them. The argument, the track record, the worldview. By the time they leave the page, they believe he's the real thing — and they know where to go next.
**Current state:** Kajabi. Functional but doesn't yet do this job well. Reel needs to be embedded when it lands.

**Architecture decision (Apr 21):** Practitioner's personal site. Person is the frame. Worldview is the content. Routing to destination sites happens at the bottom — after the argument has earned the reader's attention. Not a conversion site. No booking button at the top.

**Section structure (prototype 3 — accepted):**
Header → Hero (positioning line) → Worldview argument → Discover/Design/Deliver methodology → Origin story → Proof → Routes to destination sites → Email list (orbit) → Footer

**Routes section (bottom of page, after argument lands):**
- MC work in French → animateur.ca
- MC work in English → holdtheroom.com
- Communication course in English → Speaker Academy
- Communication course in French → Académie Prends la parole

**Currently serving double duty:** Until holdtheroom.com is built, EN MC buyers who land here see the EN reel. This is interim, not the permanent architecture.

**Open hesitations:**
- Is it primarily a credibility surface that happens to route? Or does the EN MC routing need to be more prominent while holdtheroom.com isn't built yet? Unresolved.
- Positioning line ("Most MCs live on the stage. I live in the event.") not yet live-tested with a planner. Gates the worldview section — which is most of the site.
- Origin story runs on pattern (20 years across industries) not scene. Ben Nemtin moment is raw material, not developed.
- Logo tier mixing needs curation — TELUS / iA / TEDx tier mixed with Moisson Laurentides / Canada Day tier.

### holdtheroom.com
**Platform:** Currently DNS redirect → anthonyhorng.com
**Role:** Future English MC conversion site.
**What it does (when built):** Converts an English-speaking MC buyer into an inquiry. Same job as animateur.ca but in English.
**Current state:** Placeholder. Not being built until post-MUDGIRL at earliest.

**Architecture decision (Apr 21):** EN MC conversion site. Reel is the hero. Cassandra Worthy structural logic applies here because the buyer, language, and conversion event are singular.

**Section structure (prototype 4 — accepted):**
Top bar with book button → Hero with reel embedded → Compressed argument (planner-framed) → Four service buckets → Proof (testimonials) → Three-phase process (Discover/Design/Deliver) → Logo strip → Agency/producer kit card → Qualifying inquiry form → Footer

**Four service buckets confirmed:**
Corporate events / Galas & awards / Bilingual events / Conferences & summits

**Open hesitations:**
- No build timeline. Post-MUDGIRL at earliest. May be later depending on capacity.
- Wedding: same unresolved question as animateur.ca.
- Hero headline: "The MC who holds your room so you don't have to" vs. "Most MCs live on the stage. I live in the event." — which line leads a conversion site vs. a personal site is open.

## SEO AND DISCOVERABILITY

**Current position (May 2026):**
- #4 on "animateur bilingue Québec" (Eklosion is #1)
- #1 on "bilingual MC Montreal"
- #1 on "bilingual master of ceremonies Montreal"
- Drops out of top 4 on "animateur bilingue" without geographic qualifier
- NOT cited in Google AI Overview for any relevant query

**Who IS cited in AI Overview:** Mike Tobin, Katerine-Lune Rollet, Anika Lirette, Eklosion, Scarlett Entertainment.

**Two strategic pillars (discovered May 12):**
1. Classical SEO — service-page architecture, URL slugs, title/H1/meta targeting, schema markup (BreadcrumbList + Person + LocalBusiness), geographic targeting
2. Generative SEO (AI Overview citation) — named-entity authority, topical authority content, external citations, Q&A-style content

**Status:** Two SERP analyses complete (animateur universe + maître de cérémonie universe). Discoverability spec not yet written. This is the next concrete deliverable before the rebuild.

**Competitors to watch:**
- Eklosion — agency, dominates animateur queries, has BreadcrumbList schema
- Mike Tobin — multi-domain strategy (animateurbilingue.ca + bilingualmc.ca), cited in AI Overview
- Katerine-Lune Rollet — blog topical authority, cited in AI Overview
- Randy Johnston — new entrant, clean site, no rankings (bilingual side-by-side hurts SEO — confirms two-site FR/EN split is correct)

**Wide-open opportunities:**
- English keyword universe — no Quebec specialist owns it
- Maître de cérémonie long-tail — France-dominated, Quebec content absent
