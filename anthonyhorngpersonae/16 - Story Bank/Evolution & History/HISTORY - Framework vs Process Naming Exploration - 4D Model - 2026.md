Given that your model applies to **public speaking, influence, leadership, and persuasion**, it seems you're focusing on equipping individuals with both practical tools (action steps) and conceptual guidance (principles). To best reflect this dual purpose and its focus on **growth** and **impact**, I suggest calling it a **framework** because:

1. A **framework** signals flexibility and adaptability, which is essential for leadership, influence, and persuasion—contexts that vary greatly depending on audience, goals, and settings.
2. It allows users to customize the approach to their needs, whether they're persuading a team, delivering a keynote, or inspiring action.
3. It provides a conceptual structure for reflection and growth, which is especially important for leadership and influence.

However, if you want to emphasize **how actionable it is**, you could call it a **process**.

### Recommendation: Call It a Framework

#### Name Ideas:
1. The 4D Communication and Influence Framework
2. The 4D Leadership Framework
3. The 4D Transformational Framework
4. The 4D Framework for Mastery in Communication and Influence

### Alternative: Call It a Process

#### Name Ideas:
1. The 4D Influence Process
2. The 4D Mastery Process for Communication and Leadership
3. The 4D Growth Process for Public Speaking and Leadership

### Hybrid Option

1. The 4D Framework and Process for Influence and Leadership
2. The 4D Transformational Framework: A Process for Communication Mastery
