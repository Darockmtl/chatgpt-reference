# STORY - The Dinner That Became Simple

## Raw Capture

JF lived with me as roommates. One night I made dinner — Thai chicken wings, a chicken salad. Rich, good, different. He was impressed. "Amazing. How did you do that?"

Simple. Chicken wings — dumped the sauce on. Chicken salad — half put together, then threw in artichokes because they were going to waste in the fridge.

What was once magical — *how do you do that?* — became *oh, that's easy.* The moment I showed the parts, the magic collapsed into method.

---

## Teaching Potential

The moment you show someone how something is made, the awe becomes access.

The artichokes are the key detail. They weren't a technique. They were a decision made from what was available. That's what makes it land — the messiness is real. Clean this story up and you lose everything that makes it credible.

---

## Use When

- Opening a facilitation exercise where you reveal a technique after the attempt
- Demystifying the DDD framework — showing the parts before the whole
- Sales page or webinar: the moment someone understands the system, the fear becomes curiosity
- Content: "magic collapses into simplicity the moment you show the parts"
