## The Vanessa Van Edwards Connection — Verified

Anthony noticed he was fast-nodding to try to accelerate the neighbor. The distinction Van Edwards makes:

- **Slow nodding** = tell me more, I am with you, keep going
- **Fast nodding** = finish up, hurry up, get to the point

The neighbor was misreading the fast nods — or ignoring them entirely — and leaning in harder. As Anthony physically backed up, the neighbor moved forward. The body language signals were there. They were not being received.

Source: Vanessa Van Edwards — The Daily Motivation Podcast EP 707
Quote: "Slow nodding is tell me more. Fast nodding is finish up."
Also confirmed: Jordan B. Peterson Podcast EP 565 — slow triple nod is interest and engagement, fast triple nod is impatience.

## What This Story Surfaces

1. **Storytelling is not always the answer.** When someone needs the next step, giving them the full history instead serves the teller, not the listener. Story has its place. Used without awareness of what the other person actually needs, it becomes a wall.

2. **Passion without audience awareness is not a virtue.** The neighbor was doing real work. The emotion and rhetoric were genuine. But he was not reading the room — or the body. The passion became the obstruction.

3. **Guardrail for Speaker Academy.** Anthony is a strong advocate for storytelling. This story is the internal check — the reminder that the teaching needs to include when *not* to use story, not just how to use it. Story is the mechanism of connection when the listener is open to it. When they need information to move, story becomes friction.

4. **Raw emotional documentation.** Anthony flagged this explicitly — the raw emotion of the experience is data. How it feels to be on the receiving end of unaware passion is the thing Speaker Academy students need to understand. Not as theory. As a felt experience they can recognize in themselves.
