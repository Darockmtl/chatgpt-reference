# You need structure

*Source: raw voice capture*

Building my online course took so much time and I was freezing (that's a syndrome to look up — or the psychological effect). Then I started building an AI workflow to launch the course and gave steps for priority so I wouldn't go down rabbit holes when I started being creative or insightful — then it was kept for later.

What stopped me and what I'm realizing is I didn't have a structure to keep me in boundaries and moving forwards. (Image of bouncing around in all directions.) That applies to public speaking — when preparing and not having a structure, not understanding the pieces and when they go in priority.

I had an idea for a post, got inspired, started researching how to do a good post, etc etc, then what the price should be, then how it relates to the workshop. The system brought me back to what I had to do first — where I was in the value step. And without that guardrail or structure in place you lose what is important because it's all built into each other.

## Notes

Strong analogy for teaching structure in speaking preparation — the same thing that makes course building break down makes presentation prep break down.
