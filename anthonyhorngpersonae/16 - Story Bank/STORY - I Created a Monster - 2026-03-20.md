# STORY — I Created a Monster
*Raw capture — March 20, 2026*

---

Picked up my nephew from the bus. Coming into the house he said: let me tell you, I had the worst day at school today.

I was worried. Became empathetic. Used all my limited training as a parent to let him talk so he could express himself.

He told me he was in his classroom doing bricolage — then suddenly the fire alarm went off. I was already imagining what happened. He explained it was a second grader who pulled it. Then he went into his thought process: why would someone do that? And then: I thought of losing all that I've built.

Built? What do you mean?

My friends. The memories. The things we do. All gone.

He was dramatizing — but he meant it.

Then he told me about the other classmates who were cheering, hands in the air, saying: no school. And he said: how could they even think that?

The payoff was not the fire alarm. It was what the fire alarm threatened. He did not react to the sound. He reacted to what it meant. He does not know he did that. I don't think he did it consciously the way I do today. It has become part of who he is.

That is storytelling — the way I talk to him every day. How I propose things to him to do his homework, an outing, how to reflect on what was said.

He used it against me.
