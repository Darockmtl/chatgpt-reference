# Failures Over Successes

I realized through journaling that my stories of failures are more important than my successes — because they are much more fulfilling.

When you succeed you get a momentary rush — money, credibility — but that fades.

When you fail, that is a blessing in disguise. It forces you to re-examine your steps, reassess your capabilities, reshape your mentality, and restart again. That is growth, not the complacency of winning.

Need more stories of failure.
