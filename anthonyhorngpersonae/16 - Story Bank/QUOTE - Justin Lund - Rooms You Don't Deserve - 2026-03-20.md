# QUOTE — Justin Lund — Rooms You Don't Deserve

> What kind of risk builds men? Constantly trying to be in rooms you don't deserve to be in. Apply to schools you shouldn't get into. Apply to jobs you shouldn't get into or you shouldn't receive. Express friendship with men more impressive than you. Probably the most important thing for a young man is to surround yourself with men who you perceive as being higher character and more successful than you.

**Source:** Justin Lund (video transcript)
**Use when:** The context is proximity, aspiration, risk-taking, or the idea that growth happens by reaching beyond your current level.
