# STORY — Building Speaker Academy — The Real Reason

---

I have been on stage for over twenty years.

I have hosted galas, conferences, fundraisers, product launches, award ceremonies. I have held rooms of ten people and rooms of ten thousand. I have watched speakers get their message across and watched speakers disappear behind it. I have been the person who warms up the room and the person who makes sure the message lands.

After twenty years of that, I decided to build a course.

And the first question I had to answer — the honest one, not the marketing one — was: what am I actually trying to do?

Because the easy answer is: I am going to teach people how to speak better. Give them frameworks. Give them vocal techniques. Give them stage presence tips. Wrap it in a method, put it on Kajabi, sell it.

That answer is not wrong. But it is not the real answer.

The real answer took longer to find. And it came from watching what actually happens when someone can not communicate the way they need to. Not the polished version of the problem — not the "I want to be more confident on stage" version. The real version. The one people say after they trust you enough to be honest.

They do not get the promotion because they could not articulate why they deserved it. They leave the meeting having agreed to something they did not want to agree to because they could not hold their position under pressure. They have something important to say and they swallow it because the room does not feel safe and they do not know how to make it safe. They are present but not landing. They are speaking but not being heard.

That is the real problem. And more information about speaking does not solve it.

What I realized — and this took a long time to be willing to say out loud — is that most speaking courses treat communication as a skill gap. They are not wrong that there are skills. But they are wrong about where the gap is.

The gap is not between what someone knows and what they say. The gap is between who someone is and how visible they are willing to be.

Communication is not a skill you acquire. It is a self you grow into.

That is what Speaker Academy is trying to do. Not build speakers. Remove what has been blocking one.

---

The method is called Discover, Design, Deliver. Three modules. One thread running through all of them: your message.

Discover is where you find out what you actually want to say. Not your topic. Not your expertise. Your message. The thing you believe that your audience needs to hear. Most people have never answered that question cleanly. They know their subject. They do not know their message.

Design is where you build the structure. Not because structure is elegant — because your audience deserves not to have to work to understand you. Structure is a responsibility to the listener, not a gift to yourself.

Deliver is where you execute. Not perform. Execute. There is a difference. Performance is about you. Delivery is about them.

Three stages. Each one asks a different question. Together they take someone from "I have something to say" to "I said it and it landed."

---

But here is what I have learned about building this course that I did not expect to learn.

The act of building it — of taking twenty years of instinct and embodied knowledge and making it transmissible — is its own version of Discover, Design, Deliver.

I had to discover what I actually believe about speaking, underneath all the technique. I had to design a structure that could hold it. And I have not delivered it yet — that part is coming. But the process of building it has already changed how I understand what I know.

Twenty years on stage gives you fluency. It does not automatically give you clarity about what you know or why it works. Building this course has forced that reckoning.

And the thing I keep coming back to — the line that sits at the top of every document in this project — is this:

The course does not build speakers. It removes what has been blocking one.

That is not a tagline. That is a belief about what actually needs to happen for someone to change how they communicate.

The speaker already exists. They have always existed. They just have not had the conditions to become visible yet.

That is what I am building the conditions for.
