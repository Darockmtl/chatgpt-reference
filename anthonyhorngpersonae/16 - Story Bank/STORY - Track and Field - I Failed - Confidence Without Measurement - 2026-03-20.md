# STORY — Track and Field — I Failed — Confidence Without Measurement — 2026-03-20

*Raw voice capture. Preserved verbatim.*
*See also: [[STORY - Track and Field - I Failed - Confidence Without Measurement - 2026-03-20]] (Anthony Horng Personae copy)*

---

I just have to remember the story of me being younger and doing track and field and sprinting the 100m and I thought I was pretty good and I wasn't doing bad but the funny part is that I have somebody from high school — I think his name was John, have to look it up. He said I had a certain confidence, I had speed. He was massive legs and I said that's no problem, so we did the race and guess what? Yeah, that's right. I failed.

And you know what — that confidence, I was cocky. I was confident. I knew what I was doing and I did train hard, but John was 6 foot 1 and every stride had so much length over me. I didn't even think about that. I realize now it was just that I thought I was one of the top five, and that's a reality you have to come to — you have to be able to measure where you are. It's not a failing, but you also have to analyze where you are to see where you're going to go and how you can [[INSIGHT - Preparation vs Training — The Athlete Framework for Speakers - 2026-03-03|train to become better]].

## Speaker Academy Application

**[[HEARTBEAT ARC - Course Design Framework - 2026-04-10|Discover module]]** — you cannot design a path forward without an honest read of where you are starting. Confidence without self-assessment is not preparation. The Discover phase exists precisely because most people skip this step — they [[INSIGHT - Discover Design Deliver is Cyclical Not Linear - 2026-03-19|jump to delivery]] before they understand what they are actually working with.

The story makes the point without lecturing. Anthony thought he was top five. He had trained hard. He was genuinely confident. None of that was wrong — the gap was in [[INSIGHT - From Performer to Trailblazer - The Evolution Behind the Work - 2026-03-06|what he had not measured]]. That is the same gap most speakers have when they arrive.
