## 5. Regional Venues (Outside Montreal & Quebec City)

Do not limit your outreach to just the Palais des congrès de Montréal and the Centre des congrès de Québec. Contacting the sales or events departments at these regional venues to introduce yourself as a preferred bilingual MC supplier is a strong strategy.

*   **Saint-Hyacinthe:** Centre des congrès de Saint-Hyacinthe (The **largest** convention centre in Quebec outside of Montreal and Quebec City. A massive hub for agricultural, industrial, and corporate events.)
*   **Laval:** Sheraton Laval & Centre des congrès / Centre de congrès Palace
*   **Longueuil / South Shore:** Centre des congrès de Longueuil (Hôtel Mortagne)
*   **Sherbrooke / Eastern Townships:** Sherbrooke Exhibition Centre (Palais des sports) / Delta Hotels Sherbrooke Conference Centre
*   **Trois-Rivières:** Centre des congrès de Trois-Rivières
*   **Gatineau:** Palais des congrès de Gatineau

## Next Steps for Outreach

1.  **Target the Galas First:** Prioritize events like the *Gala du Président (iA Groupe financier)*, *Les Mercuriades (FCCQ)*, and the *Banquet Reconnaissance (ACQ)*. Galas are the most natural fit for a professional MC.
2.  **Leverage LinkedIn:** For contacts where only a LinkedIn profile was found, send a concise connection request: *"Hi [Name], I'm a professional bilingual MC based in Quebec. I admire [Company]'s events and would love to connect to share my reel for any future hosting needs."*
3.  **Approach Regional Venues as Partners:** Contact the Directors of Sales at the regional venues (especially Saint-Hyacinthe and Sheraton Laval). Ask to be added to their "Preferred Suppliers" list so they can recommend you to clients booking their spaces.
