## SOURCE 1: Samaaro — LinkedIn for Speaker Sourcing (Feb 2025)
URL: https://samaaro.com/event-guide/linkedin-for-speaker-sourcing-a-guide-for-event-planners/

**How event planners actually use LinkedIn to find speakers/MCs:**
- They use advanced search filters: keywords, job titles, industries, locations
- They join relevant LinkedIn groups and monitor who is actively sharing insights
- They analyze profiles for: past speaking engagements, publications, recommendations, engagement activity
- They look for "recommendations" (testimonials) but note: "Client recommendations provided by vendors themselves are nice, but they usually are of little help in assessing overall performance because no vendor will provide you with a poor or mediocre recommendation"
- They want PERSONALIZED outreach — generic messages are ignored
- They start outreach early because "high-demand speakers often book their schedules months in advance"
- KEY INSIGHT: They engage with content BEFORE reaching out — liking, commenting, attending webinars

## SOURCE 2: Planning Pod — 4 Tactics for Evaluating Event Vendors (Dec 2013)
URL: https://planningpod.com/blog/tactics-for-evaluating-event-vendors-event-suppliers

**How event planners evaluate vendors (directly sourced):**
1. **Peer referrals first** — "The best way to know if an event vendor will follow through is to ask other planners about the vendor." Word of mouth from trusted peers is #1.
2. **Hypothetical problem scenarios** — They ask vendors: "What would you do if X went wrong?" A true professional has ready answers. If they don't, they're not prepared for a crisis.
3. **Inspect their work** — They want to see portfolio, past work, style, experience. For performance vendors: they want to SEE them in action.
4. **Contracts with performance criteria** — If they have any doubt, they put it in the contract.

**KEY INSIGHT:** "Client recommendations provided by vendors themselves are nice, but they usually are of little help." They trust PEER referrals, not testimonials on your website.

## SOURCE 3: Quebec City Business Destination — Top Challenges for Event Planners
URL: https://meetings.quebec-cite.com/en/plan-meeting-quebec-city/top-future-challenges-event-planners

**Real challenges of Quebec event planners (sourced from Quebec City Convention Centre):**
1. **Shorter lead times** — "Due to the changing profile of attendees and increased competition for their time, many organizations are rethinking their event strategies. Lead times have become significantly shorter." This means MCs need to be available on shorter notice.
2. **Booking the right venue** — Competition is fierce for good venues. This means the event planner is already stressed before they even think about the MC.
3. **Safety and security** — Risk management is top of mind.
4. **Tech savviness** — They are juggling more tech tools than ever.

## SOURCE 4: Corporate Meetings Network — Challenges Event Planners Face in 2025
URL: https://corporatemeetingsnetwork.ca/2025/02/24/challenges-event-planners-face-in-2025/
Author: Carly Silberstein, CEO of Redstone Agency (Canadian event agency)

**Real challenges of Canadian corporate event planners in 2025 (directly sourced):**
1. **Unrealistic timelines & supplier delays** — Clients pressure planners to execute high-quality events on unrealistic timelines. Suppliers have backlogs and staffing shortages.
   - Solution they use: "Building and maintaining loyalty to reliable and aligned vendors can help you receive priority treatment when turnaround times are tight."
   - **IMPLICATION FOR MC:** If you are a reliable, known vendor, you get called first when timelines are tight. Being "safe" is a competitive advantage.
2. **Rising costs & budget constraints** — "Planners are being asked to do more with less."
   - Solution: "Prioritize high-impact areas — allocate the budget to experiences that matter most to attendees."
   - **IMPLICATION FOR MC:** The MC must be positioned as a HIGH-IMPACT line item, not a nice-to-have.
3. **Attendee engagement & experience** — "With growing expectations, event planners must work harder to capture and retain audience attention."
   - **IMPLICATION FOR MC:** This is EXACTLY where a dynamic MC creates value. Frame yourself as the solution to the engagement problem.
4. **Staffing shortages & burnout** — "Burnout is a real concern." Internal teams are exhausted.
   - Solution: "Leverage trusted suppliers and freelancers to fill gaps."
   - **IMPLICATION FOR MC:** The internal event team is burned out. An external MC reduces their load. This is a real selling point.

## SOURCE 6: Eklosion FAQ — What Quebec clients ask before hiring an MC
URL: https://eklosion.ca/foire-aux-questions/

**Real questions Quebec corporate clients ask (directly sourced from FAQ page):**
- "Quelle est la différence entre un animateur professionnel et un maître de cérémonie?" — They don't know the difference. Category awareness is LOW.
- "Pourquoi faire appel à un animateur professionnel pour notre événement corporatif?" — They need to be convinced of the VALUE.
- "Quels sont les tarifs associés aux services d'un animateur professionnel?" — Price is a question, not a barrier (they ask, they don't reject).
- "À quel moment faut-il réserver vos services?" — Eklosion's answer: "Plus tôt est le mieux pour garantir la disponibilité." They note they can accommodate last-minute requests but early booking is preferred.
- "Êtes-vous capables d'adapter le ton de l'animation selon le public (formel, festif, corporatif)?" — Tone adaptability is a KEY concern for Quebec buyers.
- "Est-ce que vos animateurs sont bilingues?" — Bilingualism is a question, not an assumption.
