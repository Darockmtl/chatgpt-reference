## 2. The Language They Use (Direct Quotes from LinkedIn)

To attract these buyers, you must use their language. Here is exactly how Quebec event professionals talk about success and failure on LinkedIn.

### What Agencies Value (The "Altitude/C" Standard)
In a recent post celebrating a 20-year partnership with a vendor, the Montreal agency **Altitude/C** wrote:
> *"Vingt ans, ça se mérite. La qualité du travail, bien sûr, mais surtout ceux qui s'activent en coulisses pour que chaque détail soit à sa place et que chaque moment soit mémorable... Merci d'être un partenaire sur qui on peut compter, année après année."* [1]

**The Insight:** Agencies do not want a "star." They want a **partner they can count on**. They value people who work behind the scenes ("en coulisses") to ensure every detail is perfect. Your positioning to agencies should be: *"Je suis le partenaire sur qui vous pouvez compter."*

### What Clients Value (The "HAPPENING" Standard)
In a recent post, the agency **HAPPENING** shared a client testimonial from the Fondation Charles-Bruneau:
> *"L'équipe a été très professionnelle du début à la fin... Le jour du montage, tout s'est fait sans accroc."* [2]

**The Insight:** The ultimate compliment from a client is **"sans accroc"** (without a hitch). They want zero surprises.

He also uses the phrase **"conscience de la salle"** (room awareness), which aligns perfectly with your philosophy of moving the room.

**Your Differentiator:** Randy positions himself as a safe, adaptable host. You need to position yourself as the **intentional, dynamic message-deliverer**. You don't just keep the room safe; you *shift* the room and deliver the business value.

## 4. The Hidden Triggers: When and How They Post

**The Post-Event Celebration Signal**
Event organizers (like the Chambre de commerce et d'industrie Saguenay - Le Fjord, or RE/MAX Québec) post heavily *after* their galas. They post photos, tag sponsors, and thank their teams. 

**The Insight:** They almost never mention the MC by name in these posts unless the MC was exceptional. This means:
1. **You must post first.** After an event, you must post the photos and tag the organizer, the sponsors, and the AV team.
2. **Their posts are your prospect list.** When you see an organization post about their `#GalaReconnaissance`, you instantly know they have the budget and the need. That is your signal to connect with their Director of Communications for next year.
