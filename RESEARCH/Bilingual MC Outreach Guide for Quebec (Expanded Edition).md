## Section 4: Regional Venues (Outside Montreal & Quebec City)

Do not limit your outreach to just the Palais des congrès de Montréal and the Centre des congrès de Québec. These regional venues host significant corporate events, trade shows, and galas. Contacting the sales or events departments at these venues to introduce yourself as a preferred bilingual MC supplier is a strong strategy.

**Laval**
*   **Sheraton Laval & Centre des congrès:** The largest congress venue in Laval.
*   **Centre de congrès Palace:** A major venue for corporate events and galas.

**Longueuil / South Shore**
*   **Centre des congrès de Longueuil (Hôtel Mortagne):** The premier congress venue on the South Shore.
*   **Université de Sherbrooke - Campus Longueuil:** Hosts corporate and academic events up to 450 people.

**Sherbrooke / Eastern Townships**
*   **Sherbrooke Exhibition Centre (Palais des sports):** The largest event venue in the Sherbrooke region.
*   **Delta Hotels Sherbrooke Conference Centre:** A major hotel conference venue.

**Saint-Hyacinthe**
*   **Centre des congrès de Saint-Hyacinthe:** The **largest** convention centre in Quebec outside of Montreal and Quebec City. This is a massive hub for agricultural, industrial, and corporate events.

**Trois-Rivières & Gatineau**
*   **Centre des congrès de Trois-Rivières:** The main hub for the Mauricie region.
*   **Palais des congrès de Gatineau:** A major venue capturing both Quebec and Ottawa-based events.

## Next Steps for Outreach

1.  **Target the Galas First:** Prioritize events like the *Gala du Président (iA Groupe financier)*, *Les Mercuriades (FCCQ)*, and the *Banquet Reconnaissance (ACQ)*. Galas are the most natural fit for a professional MC.
2.  **Leverage LinkedIn:** For contacts where only a LinkedIn profile was found (e.g., Stéphanie Sörensen at Intact, Martin Robert at Beneva), send a concise connection request: *"Hi [Name], I'm a professional bilingual MC based in Quebec. I admire [Company]'s events and would love to connect to share my reel for any future hosting needs."*
3.  **Approach Regional Venues as Partners:** Contact the Directors of Sales at the regional venues (especially Saint-Hyacinthe and Sheraton Laval). Ask to be added to their "Preferred Suppliers" list so they can recommend you to clients booking their spaces.
